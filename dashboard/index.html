A MODEL YOU CAN QUESTION

Evidence, then assumptions.

01

Start with observed comparables

Use only protocols with a recorded token marker, positive market capitalization and positive TVL. Winsorize each market-cap / TVL ratio to 0.05–20× before taking medians.

—

Market cap is not fully diluted value. Token supply, revenues and prospective issuance are not in the input.

02

Reduce sparse-category overconfidence

A category's median is shrunk in log space toward the global peer median. Its own observations receive weight n / (n + 4); an empty category uses the global median.

multiple = exp(w · ln(category median)
+ (1 − w) · ln(global median))

The 4-observation prior strength is a disclosed modeling assumption, not a measured fact.

03

Adjust growth and tracking age

Seven-day TVL growth is capped at −50% to +100%. The age input is days since DefiLlama began tracking, measured against the sealed date, capped at three years.

value = TVL × peer multiple
× (1 + 0.25 · capped growth)
× (0.75 + 0.25 · age / 1095)

Missing growth → 0%; missing age → factor 0.875. Both are surfaced and widen the scenario band.

04

Show uncertainty and exclusions

Bands use shrunk peer quartiles with a minimum span of 0.65–1.5× the center. Sparse categories widen another 25%; each missing input adds 25%.

SCENARIO RANGE ≠ CONFIDENCE INTERVAL

Recorded tokens are separate. Wrapped, exchange, bridge and versioned products are filtered. Outside the watchlist, parent-protocol independence cannot be verified.
